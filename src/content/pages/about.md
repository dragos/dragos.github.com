I'm a software developer passionate about Scala, and programming languages in general. I currently work for [Databricks](https://www.databricks.com). You can read about some of the cool stuff I've been working on in this [blog post](https://www.databricks.com/blog/open-sourcing-metals-v2-databricks-java-and-scala-language-server-multi-million-line-codebases).

Before that, I founded [Triplequote](https://web.archive.org/web/20230724205946/https://www.triplequote.com/) (acquired by [Develocity](https://develocity.ai/blog/sbt-build-observability-troubleshooting-develocity/)) together with Mirco Dotta, where we built a parallel Scala compiler (called Hydra).

Before that, during my time with Typesafe (later, Lightbend and now akka.io), I lead the Eclipse Scala IDE for about 4 years.

Before that, I got my PhD at EPFL, under the supervision of prof. Martin Odersky.
